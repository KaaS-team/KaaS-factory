## Issues

### #606 Monthly budget per team stops intake at the cap

## Problem

Spend is capped for the whole factory, not per team, so one team can use another team's budget.

## Acceptance

- A team has a monthly cap in its config.
- A team over its cap stops taking new work, and the inbox says why.
- Other teams are unaffected.
- A test covers a team over and a team under its cap.

## Out of scope

- Billing.

Source: internal research on team models.

### #607 Egress and secrets policy: a build calling an unlisted host is blocked

## Problem

A build can call any host and read any secret in its environment. Teams need to say which hosts and secrets a run may use.

## Acceptance

- A policy lists allowed hosts and secrets per team.
- A call to an unlisted host is blocked and named in the run.
- A secret that is not listed is absent from the run environment.
- Tests cover an allowed host, a blocked host and an unlisted secret.

## Out of scope

- Network sandboxes for the whole machine.

### #612 Composite identity, approval chains and autonomy levels

## Problem

Approval is one person pressing merge. Teams need an agent and a person recorded together, a chain of approvers for sensitive paths and a setting for how much the factory may do alone.

## Acceptance

- Each action records the agent and the person it acted for.
- A path can require a chain of approvers, in order.
- Autonomy has three levels: suggest, change with approval, and merge on green.
- Tests cover each level and a chain with a missing approver.

## Out of scope

- Single sign-on.
