## Issues

### #393 Automatic model routing per step, within team policy

## Problem

Every step uses one model. A cheap model could do triage and the change summary, while planning and review need a stronger one. There is no per-step choice, and a team cannot say which models are allowed.

## Acceptance

- Each workflow step has a model, chosen from team policy, with an `auto` setting that picks by step.
- A model outside the team's allow list is refused at dispatch, and the refusal names the model and the list.
- The default setting keeps today's behaviour.
- Tests cover `auto` per step, an allowed model and a refused one.

## Out of scope

- Per-run budgets: the cost guard already refuses a run over its estimate.

Source: internal research on model routing.
