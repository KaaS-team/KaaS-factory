### #328 Measure blast-radius selector misses after merge

## Problem

The change selector picks which checks a change reaches, but nobody measures what it misses. The miss rate says whether the selector can be trusted. A script that finds misses exists (`scripts/affected-misses.py`); nothing runs it after merge.

## Acceptance

- A post-merge job runs the full gate on main and records each failure the selector would have skipped.
- A miss opens an issue that names the check and the files changed.
- The job reports the miss rate over the last runs.
- A test proves a skipped check that fails produces a miss row.

## Out of scope

- Editing the impact map automatically. The workflow is staged as a CI file for a person to move.
