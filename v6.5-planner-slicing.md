## Issues

### #392 Planner slices work into ~30 minute epics with one PR each and caps dependency chains

## Why
Specs that depend on one another cannot run in parallel, so a queue of 24 items shrinks to a few runnable ones until the first merges. Three specs waited on one resync fix, and several more on one API change.

## Change
`factory plan` slices work into runnable units, each about 30 minutes of execution and one feature, with these rules:
1. Items that share a dependency chain become one epic with ordered tasks and one PR, not separate PRs that wait.
2. A task that only needs a small piece of another (an interface, a type) takes that piece into its own PR instead of waiting for the whole.
3. Items with disjoint Files and no dependency run at once; the planner reports the longest dependency chain and warns above two.
4. A unit above the size cap is split at a task boundary, never mid-task.

## Acceptance
Given a fixture backlog with a three-deep chain, the planner emits one epic. A chain above the cap is split at a task boundary. A test walks the planner over the fixture and asserts the longest chain is at most two.

Relates to #383, #378, #227.
