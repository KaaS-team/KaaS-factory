Where does your team's delivery choke once agents write the code?

When agents make code cheap, the bottleneck moves to review, verification and release. DORA's 2025 report found AI amplifies the system it lands in: strong teams get faster, weak ones get less stable. Score each row 0 to 3 with the team in the room, then pick the three lowest rows that block the most work.
