AI-Native Software Factory Buildcamp, 19 Oct to 7 Nov 2026

Your worksheets for three projects in three weeks

Each week you build one project on your own repository: your workflow, your blueprint, then your Software Factory. Each sheet asks six short questions, and you answer them in your own words.

How to use them

  1. Open a sheet on a laptop and write your answers. Plain text, bullets and Markdown tables all work, and you can paste notes from anywhere.
  2. Your answers save in this browser as you type, so you can close the tab and come back.
  3. When you are done, press Save as PDF or Download .md and post the file in the cohort before the due date.
