AI-Native Software Factory

Build your Software Factory on your own repository

Three worksheets, in order: map your workflow, write your blueprint, then run your Software Factory. Each one keeps the work short and the same for everyone, so live time goes to discussing your workflows rather than explaining forms.

How to use them

  1. Open a sheet on a laptop in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
  2. Type into any box. It saves in that browser as you type, so you can close the tab and come back.
  3. Press Save as PDF to share a PDF, or Download .md to share your answers as Markdown.

The worksheets, in order

Step 1Your workflowMap one real change, count your ticket mix, redesign it as stages with checks and risk tiers. With step 1Workflow cardsOne page per ticket type: trigger, steps, human in the loop, stop rules, evidence. Step 2Your blueprintRules, skills, hooks, gates and the PR evidence contract, mapped to files. Step 3Your Software FactoryIsolation, decomposition, the Doer and Tester loop, metrics and your demo card.
