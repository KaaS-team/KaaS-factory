AI-Native Software Factory Buildcamp · 19 Oct to 7 Nov 2026

Your worksheets for three projects in three weeks

Each week you build one project on your own repository: your workflow, your blueprint, then your Software Factory. The worksheets keep the homework short and the same for everyone, so we spend live time discussing your workflows rather than explaining forms. Open a sheet, type into the boxes, then use Save as PDF and post the PDF in the cohort.

How to use them

  1. Open a sheet on a laptop in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
  2. Type into any box. It saves in that browser as you type, so you can close the tab and come back.
  3. Press Save as PDF (or Ctrl or Cmd + P), choose Save as PDF as the destination, and post the PDF in the cohort before the due date.
