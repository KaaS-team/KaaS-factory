Step 2: your blueprint

Decide what your agents know, may do and must prove

Your blueprint is the set of files in your repository that make every agent run the same way: rules, skills, commands, hooks and gates. Commit the files as you go; this sheet is the map of what you committed.

1Map your .claude directory

What loads every session costs context on every task, so keep it short. What loads on demand can be long.
