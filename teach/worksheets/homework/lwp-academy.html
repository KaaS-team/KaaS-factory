Homework: lwp-academy

lwp-academy holds the money, so the factory must stop at the till

Since 19 October the academy serves auth, Pro checkout, the Stripe webhook and the billing portal in-process, on a Postgres member database. That makes it the riskiest of the three, and today it has the thinnest guard: no .claude folder, no AGENTS.md, no pre-push hook and no CI.

7The factory for lwp-academy

Isolation
Stripe and auth secrets never in the stage env. Tests use the compose Postgres on tmpfs, so every run starts empty.
Routes
Only content, copy sync, redirects and gate fixes. Everything else stays in sessions.
Metric
Share of commits that came through a factory PR, and zero factory PRs touching a protected path.
Demo card
Change a price name in the Maven plan, let the factory turn make maven green, read the diff live.
