Week 3, your Software Factory. Questions 1 to 3 by Wed 4 Nov, 4 to 6 for demo day on Sat 7 Nov.

Run your blueprint on a real issue, end to end

Your factory runs your week 1 workflow with your week 2 blueprint, in isolation and with limits, and stops at a pull request a person merges.

1Where does each run happen, and what can it reach?

Where it runs, the files and commands it may use, its network, the secrets it gets, and its limits on time and spend.

2Break one real plan into tasks you can check one at a time

For each task, list the files, the check that proves it and what it depends on.

3How do the doer and the tester loop, and when do they stop?

The tester is a second agent with a fresh context. It runs the checks and sends back failures the doer can act on. Every loop ends: done, ask a person, or stop.

4What changed, in numbers?

Compare with week 1: time to pull request, review wait, review rounds, cost per pull request and reverts. Name the number that tightens approvals when it gets worse.

5Your three-minute demo

The issue in one line, what happened at each stage, one thing the factory caught or refused, and the pull request link.

6What will you automate next?

The next ticket type, and the first gate it needs.
